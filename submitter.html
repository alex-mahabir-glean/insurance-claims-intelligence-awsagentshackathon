How to File Your Claim

Filing Process

  • Answer the AI assistant's questions about your claim type and incident details
  • Provide information about the date, location, and circumstances of the incident
  • Upload any supporting documents (photos, police reports, receipts, etc.)
  • Review all your information carefully before submitting
  • Receive your claim ID and estimated review timeline

Tips for Success

  • Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident
  • Have all relevant documents ready before starting
  • Take clear photos of any damage or evidence
  • Keep your claim ID for future reference
